About these figures. The input values come from BindingScores_StatisticalAnalysis.xlsx, cross-checked against raw KD from All_Species_BindingScores.xlsx. Figure 15 correlations were recalculated and checked against all 108 entries in the supplied correlation CSV. The blue, grey, magenta and olive-green model-identity colours follow thesis Figure 20 (and structural benchmarking figures); heatmap gradients are unchanged. Clustering uses Euclidean distance/complete linkage for Figures 14 and 16, and distance (1 − ρ)/complete linkage for Figure 15. Original Figure 14 AU bootstrap labels are not shown because the corresponding saved pvclust objects and edge tables were not provided. Tree leaf orientation can differ from the published R figure while preserving the clustering structure. Data and methods: validation report · all 432 scores · species medians · cross-species correlations.
